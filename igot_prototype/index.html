Lecture Description & Learning Objectives

This flagship lecture series, delivered by Prof. Steve Brunton and adopted by the National Statistical Systems Training Academy (NSSTA), bridges theoretical probability with applied modern data analysis. Learners study how empirical data is structured, cleaned, and statistically characterized for policy decisions.

Core Competencies Covered
  • Probability Density Functions (PDF) & Cumulative Distributions (CDF)
  • Sampling Variance & Neyman Optimum Allocation
  • Central Limit Theorem in NSS Survey Rounds
  • Regression Grounding & Residual Variance Analysis
Official Cadre Relevance
  • Junior Statistical Officers (JSO) & Senior Statistical Officers (SSO)
  • National Accounts Division (NAD) - Macro Computations
  • Field Operations Division (FOD) - Survey Rounds
  • Price & Index Compilation Teams (CPI / IIP)
